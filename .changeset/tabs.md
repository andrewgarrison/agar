Add Tabs (`@agar/react/tabs`): `Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab` and `TabsPanel`, composable parts over UI SDK's tabs, with the dock's tint jelly springing to the selected tab. It tugs toward a pressed tab, leans toward a hovering mouse, and supports vertical lists, manual activation and typed values. A plain CSS pill marks the selection until the jelly is ready, without a canvas, and in forced-colours mode.

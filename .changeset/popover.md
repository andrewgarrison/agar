Add Popover (`@agar/react/popover`): `Popover`, `PopoverTrigger`, `PopoverContent`, `PopoverTitle`, `PopoverDescription` and `PopoverClose`, composable parts over UI SDK's popover. A glass jelly grows out of the trigger as the panel opens, follows its size, and shrinks back as it closes, however it closes, controlled closes included. It supports placement, controlled state, modal and non-dismissible popovers. A plain panel shows until the jelly is ready, without a canvas, and in forced-colours mode.
